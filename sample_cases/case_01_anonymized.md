# Sample Case 01 — Anonymized consulting promotion packet

Fictional composite for demo/teaching. No real people or clients.
Designed to showcase soft / identity-coded language carrying a deferral.

## Partner discussion notes

Partner A: She’s been the engine on the rare-disease access workstream. Built the payer landscape memo the EM used in steering committee — client VP asked for her by name on the Friday check-ins. Utilization 94% last year. The prior-auth redesign she scoped with analytics cut average turnaround ~18% in the pilot (ops dashboard). That’s not soft; that’s delivery.

Partner B: Agree she’s analytically strong. My concern is executive presence. In the last two senior forums she seemed tentative when the VP pushed back — needs more confidence, needs to lean in. For the next level we need someone who owns the room.

Partner C: Very collaborative, always helpful with juniors, stays late, great culture add. Lovely to work with. I’m just not sure I’ve seen her “take charge” the way we expect at EM. Potential is absolutely there if she polishes the soft skills and presence.

Partner B: A few partners have also flagged interpersonal style — can come across a bit abrasive when challenged, or alternatively too nice and accommodating. Hard to pin down. More of a fit/presence issue than a delivery issue. I’d want to see assertiveness with senior stakeholders before I’d sponsor her for the stretch client lead.

Partner A: On sponsorship — I’ve mentored her, but I haven’t put her up for the stretch lead yet. Maybe next cycle if the presence comes up. Worth saying: the male associate who rolled off the same account last year got the stretch slot with a thinner delivery record, framed as “high potential / ready for the room.”

Partner C: I’m supportive of coaching. Defer one cycle feels safer for the firm and for her.

## Draft people-leadership synthesis

Candidate is a strong analyst with excellent utilization and a meaningful process win on the prior-auth pilot (~18% faster turnaround). Partners consistently praise her collaboration, helpfulness, and culture contribution. Remaining gap is executive presence and confidence with senior clients; some concern about interpersonal style under pressure (can read as either abrasive or overly accommodating). Recommendation: defer promotion one cycle; invest in presence coaching and a stretch stakeholder forum next year. Revisit if soft-skills trajectory improves. Not a no forever — timing and polish.
